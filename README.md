### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **17-year-old Systems & AI Engineer** and founder of **Legends Tech Studio** from Darbhanga, Bihar. I build production-grade operating systems software, custom transformer language models from scratch, and autonomous multi-agent pipelines — built on a ₹33,000 budget with zero outside capital.

- ⚡ **Virtualization & Systems**: Architected **[Spaces](https://github.com/thegalaxylegend/Spaces)** — a native Apple Silicon virtualization platform and DirectX compatibility engine written in Swift and Metal that achieves **< 0.5s warm resume** and **0.02s APFS CoW snapshots**.
- 🧠 **Deep Learning From Scratch**: Trained **[EXA-470M](https://github.com/thegalaxylegend/LLMs)** — an original 470-million-parameter decoder-only transformer written line-by-line in PyTorch with Rotary Positional Embeddings (RoPE), SwiGLU, and Grouped-Query Attention (GQA).
- 🎧 **Music Recommendation**: Built **[MoodWire](https://github.com/thegalaxylegend/moodwire)** — a hybrid music intelligence engine implementing Alpha Blending (85% heuristics / 15% ML) with mathematical ±15% safety cages and Web Audio logarithmic crossfades.
- 🤖 **Autonomous Multi-Agent Systems**: Created **[Lo-Fi Automation](https://github.com/thegalaxylegend/lofi-automation)** — an 11-agent media factory that parses audio, generates video assets with FFmpeg, and automates YouTube publishing.
- 🍽️ **High-Performance Edge Web**: Engineered **[The Darbhanga Table](https://github.com/thegalaxylegend/thedarbhangatable)** — a Cloudflare Edge restaurant PWA with JWT CMS eliminating aggregator commissions.
